## Bug 4: WorldFusion gRPC payload too large (~5MB)

Tyler had noted the `pickled_features` gRPC field should be compressed. The proto comment
even said `// Compressed intermediate features` but neither send nor receive had a compression
call. `ecloud_actor_client.py` did raw `msgpack.packb`; `sim_api.py` did raw `msgpack.unpackb`.
For a float16 `spatial_features` BEV tensor the msgpack output is ~5MB, exceeding gRPC's
default 4MB message limit and making WorldFusion distributed non-functional.

Fix: `zlib.compress()` wrapping `msgpack.packb` on send; `zlib.decompress()` before
`msgpack.unpackb` on receive. stdlib, no new dep. Expected ~2-3x compression on float16
activation maps. `[UNPACK FEATURES] ... bytes` log line shows compressed size.

**Result:** Both BM2CP (late fusion) and WorldFusion (intermediate fusion) confirmed working
distributed by end of session.
