## Key Decisions

- **Obstacle-triggered handoff (jrapp decision, plan mode):** Scenario B trigger is the
  fast NPC crossing the locale boundary (not the ego). This required the obstacle
  export/import extension. Current `export_vehicle_state` guards on VehicleManager
  and is not usable for NPC obstacles.
- **SyncArrival for NPC timing (jrapp, post-plan):** used CARLA scenario runner's
  `SyncArrival` atomic to coordinate NPC arrival at x=220 (locale 1, left lane) with
  ego arrival at x=250 (30m from emergency vehicle). Deterministic timing regardless
  of ego acceleration profile; NPC continues at 22 m/s (80 km/h) after sync point.
- **start_actors.sh sequential mode:** the existing script always appended `-d` or `-eo`
  to the base process. Sequential scenarios (ScenarioRunner subprocess) need neither.
  Added as a first-class mode rather than a workaround.
