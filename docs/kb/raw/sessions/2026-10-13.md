## Decisions

- The rule runs at the sender, before the payload is built, as the report specifies; the edge merge is the plain late-fusion AB3DMOT with anchoring forced off. SBA code untouched.
- Senders see beacons through a recorded per-tick broadcast history with a delay and max-age window (defaults 0 and 2 ticks), propagated under constant velocity, own beacon excluded.
